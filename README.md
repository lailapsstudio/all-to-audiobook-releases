# All to Audiobook

All to Audiobook turns the books and documents you already have into audiobook files you keep.

This repository has the downloads for All to Audiobook Studio, the desktop app for Mac and Windows. The website is at [alltoaudiobook.com](https://alltoaudiobook.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=launch).

## Two ways to use it

The website reads EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT and Markdown files in your browser, in English. Studio is a desktop app for Mac and Windows. It makes the audio on your own computer, in 5 languages, and it can give each character a voice of their own.

### The website

Open an ebook, PDF or document, pick a chapter to start from, choose a voice, and listen. Your file is never uploaded.

- Free makes up to three hours of audio, once per account, as MP3 files.
- Pro has no length limit and makes one M4B audiobook with chapters and a cover. It adds 11 voices, the footnote and caption choices, and a pronunciation list.
- Your files, text and audio stay on your computer. The page never uploads them.

### Studio

All to Audiobook Studio is the desktop app for Mac and Windows. It makes the audio on your own computer, in 5 languages with 21 voices.

- **Your own voice:** Add an audio file of you reading, with at least 10 seconds of speech, and Studio makes a voice from it. Use only your own voice or the voice of someone who agreed.
- **A voice for each character:** Give each character in a book a voice of their own, up to 8 in a book. The narrator reads the rest.
- **Check and fix passages:** Studio listens to each passage it makes and marks the ones with missing words, so you know which to hear again. Change the speed, the pause or the voice of any passage, or make it again.
- **Audiobook files:** Export one M4B audiobook with chapters and a cover, or one MP3 file for each chapter. The volume is even across all chapters.
- **Books and documents:** Open an EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown file. Studio finds the chapters, and an EPUB brings its title, author and cover.

Studio includes Pro and the desktop app for Mac and Windows.

## Questions

**Can I try Studio before I buy?**
Yes. Download Studio and log in: every account can make 30 minutes of audio free. Get Studio to export your audiobooks.

**I already have Pro. Do I pay the full price?**
No. You pay only the difference, because Studio includes Pro.

**On how many computers can I use Studio?**
On 2 computers.
